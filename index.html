How it works

From guest list to "welcome, come on in" — four steps.

1

Create your event

Pick an event type, add the date, venue and a cover photo — or design your own card.

2

Add your guests

Add them one by one, import from Excel, or pull straight from your contacts. Each gets a unique QR invitation.

3

Send & collect RSVPs

Share by SMS or WhatsApp. Guests tap a link — no login, no app download — and let you know if they're coming.

4

Check guests in

Scan each QR code at the door. Alika keeps count in real time, including single vs. double invitations.

Why hosts use Alika

Everything an organizer actually needs.

Effortless guest lists

Add guests manually, import an Excel sheet, or pull them from your contacts — with automatic phone validation and duplicate detection.

Personalized invitations

Every guest gets their own card with their name and a unique QR code, single or double invitation, ready to share instantly.

No-login RSVPs

Guests confirm with one tap on a link you send — no account, no app install. Their answer lands straight in your guest list.

QR check-in scanner

Scan guests in at the door. Double invitations are handled correctly — 1 of 2 today, top up to 2 of 2 later.

Live reports

See accepted, declined and checked-in counts update in real time, and export a shareable PDF report whenever you need one.
